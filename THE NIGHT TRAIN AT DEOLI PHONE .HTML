Moral of The Night Train at Deoli

  1. Some memories remain beautiful because they are incomplete.
    The narrator never discovers what happened to the basket-selling girl. By leaving the mystery unsolved, he preserves the beauty of his memories and dreams.
  2. Love and human connection can arise unexpectedly.
    A brief meeting between two strangers leaves a deep and lasting impact on the narrator's life, showing that even short encounters can be meaningful.
  3. Sometimes hope is stronger than reality.
    The narrator chooses to keep hoping and imagining rather than risk learning a disappointing truth. This shows how people often cherish their dreams and possibilities.
  4. Not every story has a definite ending.
    Life does not always provide answers or closure. Some questions remain unanswered, and we learn to live with uncertainty.
